Getting started

  1. On first launch, add a person. For anyone under 20, give the birth month and year, so the app can show the growth chart instead of a weight goal.
  2. Tap the button at the bottom. The camera opens: take a photo or a short video of your scale's display, or tap From gallery.
  3. Check the number, change it with − and + if needed, and save. Your chart, streak and milestones update at once.

Settings are behind the gear at the top right: people, colours, units, language, the daily reminder, the full version and your data.

Questions and answers

The app didn't read the number, or read it wrong.

Make sure the display is lit, fills a good part of the picture and has no glare on it. A short video sometimes works better than a photo, because the display can change while it settles. You can always set the number yourself with − and + before saving.

What does ✓ VERIFIED mean?

The number the app read in your photo matches the weight you saved. It's a small sign of trust, for example on a share card. Entries without it count exactly the same.

Do my photos leave my phone?

No. The number is read on your device, and photos and videos stay in the app's storage on your phone. A photo only leaves it when you share a card yourself.

How do I add another person, such as my child?

Open Settings and tap Add person under People. You can have up to five people. During the 14-day trial this is open to everyone; after that, more than one person needs the full version.

How do I change kilograms to pounds, or the colours?

Open Settings. Under the look section you'll find Colors (White, Black or Auto, which follows your phone), Unit (kilograms, pounds or stones) and Language (English or German).

The daily reminder doesn't show up.

Turn it on in Settings under Daily reminder and pick the times. Then check that notifications for Taskamento are allowed in your phone's settings.

What is free, and what does the full version add?

Logging with photo, the chart, BMI and the growth chart need no purchase. For the first 14 days you can also try several people, share cards and milestones. One purchase keeps them for good and adds the PDF report for your doctor. There is no subscription and no advertising.

I bought the full version. How do I get it back on a new phone?

Open Settings, go to Full version and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account or Google account you bought it with. Your entries don't move to the new phone; see the next answer.

Is my data backed up or synced?

No. Everything is stored only on this phone: there's no account, no cloud and no sync. Deleting the app deletes all entries and photos, and reinstalling starts empty. To keep a record, make a PDF report (full version) and save it somewhere safe.

How do I delete my data?

To delete one person with all their entries, photos and milestones, open that person in Settings and delete them. To delete everything, use Delete all data in Settings. Both can't be undone.

How does the growth chart for children work?

For people under 20, the app shows weight-for-age percentile curves based on the CDC growth charts, and no weight goal. Percentiles are general reference values, not a diagnosis. Talk to your doctor about your child's weight.

Which phones does it run on?

iPhone with iOS 16 or later, and Android phones with Android 7.0 or later. The app is in English and German.
