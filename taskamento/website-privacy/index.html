Privacy

The short version: this website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. The app keeps everything on your phone: no account, no ads, no analytics, and no server of ours that receives your data.

The Taskamento app

  • On your phone only: people, weight entries, notes and the photos or videos of your scale are stored in the app on your phone. We receive none of it.
  • No account, no tracking: the app has no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics.
  • Sharing: a share card or PDF report leaves the app only when you share it yourself.
  • Purchase: the one-time purchase is handled by Apple or Google. We receive no payment details.

No cookies, no analytics, no tracking

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking tools. Every page, picture and stylesheet comes from this website itself.
