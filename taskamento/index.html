Taskamento

One page. Two colours.
Your weight, nothing else.

Step on the scale, take a photo of the display, and the app reads the number for you. Your weight, chart, streak and milestones update at once. No feeds, no ads, no account.

Photo proof

Photograph your scale. The app reads it.

One button at the bottom: log today's weight. It opens the camera straight away.

  • Take a photo or a short video of your scale, or pick one from your library
  • The number on the display is read on your device
  • If the number in the photo matches what you saved, the entry gets a ✓ VERIFIED tag
  • Photos are shown in black and white and stay on your phone

One page

Everything you need, nothing to scroll.

Your latest weight, the change since your last log and since you started, and a chart for 7 days, 1 month, 3 months, 1 year or all time, with a 7-day average line.

  • 84.5Start
  • 81.7Lowest
  • 25.2BMI
  • 4 dStreak

Below them: your goal bar and your milestones. Tap LIST to see every entry.

Share your progress

Share the progress, keep the number.

  • A clean black-and-white card with your weight, photo, goal progress and last 7 days
  • You can hide the exact weight and share only your progress
  • Your name is never on the card

Milestones

Forty milestones and a pixel dog.

  • 40 milestones, one for every 0.5 kg (1.1 lb) on the way to your goal
  • A daily streak for logging
  • A small pixel dog that breathes, wags, barks and jumps when you log

The milestone pictures are the only colour in the app.

For adults and children

A goal for grown-ups. A growth chart for kids.

  • Adults: goal bar and a true-scale BMI bar with history
  • Children and teens under 20: weight-for-age percentile curves based on CDC growth charts, with no weight goal
  • Several profiles on one phone

Details

The small things.

  • Units

    Kilograms, pounds or stones; centimetres or feet and inches.

  • Appearance

    White, black or automatic, following your phone.

  • Reminder

    A daily reminder at a time you choose.

  • PDF report

    For your doctor, with the chart and all entries (full version).

  • Profiles

    Up to five people on one phone, children included.

  • Languages

    English and German.

Privacy

Everything is stored on your device. Taskamento has no account, no analytics and no advertising, and it sends none of your data anywhere. Privacy policy

Taskamento is a logging tool and does not give medical advice. BMI and growth percentiles are general reference values. Talk to a doctor about questions on your own or your child's weight.
