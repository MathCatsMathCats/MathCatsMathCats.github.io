The job

Forty turns of low tide.

You are a salvager. A dealer on the mainland pays well for the Saint's Bell, kept in Saltmarrow Keep, a fortress on a tidal island. You row out when the sea pulls back.

Every move costs a turn. After about forty, the tide is back, the stairs are under water, and anyone still inside stays there.

The tide bar fills as you play.

The voice

Your narrator is lying.

A voice describes every wet stone and offers advice. It is sarcastic, oddly friendly, gives you a nickname, and seems to like you. It also slips eight lies into what it tells you, and it knows more about why you took this job than anyone on the mainland.

“I would walk right by. They told the harbour that prisoner killed the last salvager.”

That's not true.

When something doesn't add up, call it out, straight from the story or from your Field notes. Catch a lie and hidden paths open. Doubt the wrong thing and the voice sulks, or goes quiet.

How it plays

Read, choose, or type your own.

  • Tap a choice, or type what you want to do in your own words.
  • Fourteen hand-written rooms in three acts, from the Landing down to the Bell Chamber.
  • Carry what matters: an ash oar, spare keys, a brass candle.
  • Free Wren, the salvager chained in the gatehouse, or leave them there. Wren knows the voice lies.
  • Every run ends with a Salvage Report: how you got out, what happened to the Bell and to Wren, and how many lies you caught.

What's inside

Quiet horror, built to replay.

  • A narrator who lies

    Eight lies hidden in the story. Catch them to open paths and learn the truth about the keep.

  • Chill horror

    Creeping and quiet: no gore and no jump scares. A few moments are meant to unsettle.

  • Six endings

    Get out, get trapped, or something stranger. A run takes about an hour, and every choice changes the next one.

  • Honest narrator

    Finish the story and unlock a replay where the voice has to tell the truth. Through gritted teeth.

  • Works offline

    The whole story plays with no internet, no account and no sign-in. Nothing to set up.

  • Seven languages

    English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Polish and Russian.

Optional

Bring your own AI.

Out of the box, Low Tide plays fully offline with hand-written scenes and lines. If you have your own key for Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic, you can add it under Narrator & API, and the voice improvises live and answers anything you type.

  • Optional: leave it off and play the whole game offline.
  • Your key stays on your phone. Requests go straight from your phone to the provider you chose.
  • We run no server, so we never see your key or what you type.
