Privacy

The short version: this website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. The game needs no account, shows no ads, and sends nothing to us.

The Low Tide app

  • Offline by default: with the standard narration source, the game makes no network requests at all.
  • On your phone only: your saved expedition, endings and settings stay in the app on your phone.
  • Your own AI key (optional): if you add one, the story context and what you type go straight from your phone to the AI provider you chose. We run no server and never see it.
  • Purchase: handled by Apple or Google. We receive no payment details.
