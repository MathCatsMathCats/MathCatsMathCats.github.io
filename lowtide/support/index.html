Getting started

  1. On the title screen, tap New expedition.
  2. Answer a few questions before you row out: your name, what you're afraid of, and why you took this job. Then tap Begin.
  3. Read, then tap a choice or type what you want to do. Keep an eye on the tide bar at the top: each move costs a turn.

Field notes (the book icon at the top) shows the lies you've caught, what you carry, who is with you and the map. Narrator & API (the sliders icon) holds the narrator settings.

Questions and answers

Do I need the internet or an account?

No. The whole story plays offline with no account and no sign-in. Narration source Offline (Backup lines) is the default.

How do I catch a lie?

When something the voice says doesn't fit, tap That's not true. when it appears, or point at the detail in your Field notes. There are eight lies. Accusing the voice of things that are true has consequences too.

What happens when the tide comes in?

You have about forty turns. When the tide is fully back, anyone still inside the keep is trapped. Row back from the Landing in time to get out.

How do I use my own AI key?

Open Narrator & API, choose Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic as the narration source, paste your own API key and save. The voice then improvises live and answers free text. Usage is billed to you by that provider. To go back, choose Offline (Backup lines).

Is my API key safe?

It is stored only in the app's own storage on your phone and sent only to the provider you chose. We have no server and never see it. You can clear it in Narrator & API at any time.

What's free, and what is the full keep?

Act 1 is free: the Landing, the Gatehouse, the Courtyard and the Wardens' Lodge. The full keep (Acts 2 and 3, all six endings and the honest narrator) is planned as a one-time purchase. No subscription, no ads.

I bought the full keep. How do I get it on a new phone?

Use Restore purchase on the unlock screen, signed in with the same Apple Account or Google account you bought it with. Your saved expedition stays on the old phone.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your phone. There is no cloud save. Deleting the app deletes your saved expedition and endings.

How do I change the language?

Low Tide follows your phone's language when it is one of seven: English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Polish or Russian. Otherwise it uses English.

Is it scary? Who is it for?

It is quiet horror: creeping atmosphere, no gore, no jump scares, no swearing. It is rated 12+.

Which phones does it run on?

iPhone and iPad, and Android phones and tablets. Exact minimum versions will be listed in the stores at launch.

Contact
